# SPEC: Newsroom

## Changelog

- **1.12 (2026-11-22)** — **Well-known sources for a brief topic** (ADR-0076). `GET|POST /admin/api/newsroom/briefs/sources` and `DELETE .../sources/{id}`: the built-in catalog (`sources/catalog.go`, feeds and publisher searches, each feed fetched and parsed when written) then the owner's own list (`my_sources` in `briefs.json`, at most 100, refused when it repeats an entry). A topic copies the pick with its name (`label`). The topic form's "Follow a website" is a chip box with suggestions while typing and a "Browse well-known sources" list; "My sources" is a card on Set up. A pasted feed link in neither list is read once with the source look-up and shown with what it gives or its problem (a hint; the topic still saves), a page that is not a feed offers the feeds it advertises, and a found feed can be kept in "My sources". The look-up routes now refuse only when Watches and Briefs are both off (they refused when Watches alone was off, which would have broken the check on a Briefs-only install). **"Write it now" first collects** the edition's topics never read or older than its interval (`Collector.CollectStale`); an edition section that was never read says so.
- **1.11 (2026-11-22)** — **Briefs tab** on the Newsroom page (`/newsroom/briefs`): Read (the edition as last written: top stories, a foldable section per topic with its note and unread sources, headline-only and update tags, "Write it now") and Set up (topics: name, searches, feeds, and under Advanced category, region, words to leave out; editions: name, ready by, days, topics, and under Advanced how often and how long before it collects). Reading asks nothing of the model. A topic's Hacker News and Mastodon sources made by the assistant are kept when the form saves. It also has the **Model calls** setting (`GET|PUT .../briefs/settings`: the daily limit of model calls, empty for the default 200, 1 to 2000; saves only that one field of the `newsroom:` block). Not built: the dashboard card, Your watches/Home/Your day, Telegram.
- **1.10 (2026-11-22)** — **The edition is written.** The Briefs module composes each edition once per ready time, starting 20 minutes ahead (catching up for up to 3 hours after a stop): per section it caps the candidates (15, best by outlets, none older than 48 h), asks the model to choose up to 5 and word them, keeps only entries that cite a shown story, and makes one more call for the top 4. A headline-only story keeps its own headline and gets no summary. A story shown in the last 24 h is not shown again unless more outlets report it (then it is labelled an update). A missing, spent or wrong model leaves the section ranked by outlets under the stories' own headlines, with the reason in the section's note; the edition always ships. An edition is stored (30 days, 100 at most). New routes: `POST .../briefs/editions/{id}/issue` (make now), `GET .../briefs/issues?edition=`, `GET .../briefs/issues/{id}`. No screen, Telegram or Your watches/Home/Your day yet.
- **1.9 (2026-11-22)** — The stack's daily model-call limit is the owner's setting `model_calls_per_day` in the `newsroom:` block of settings.yml (default 200, ceiling 2000, read at every call, shared by watches and briefs). The model port stays on while either module is on. Its screen field is under Briefs, Set up (1.11).
- **1.8 (2026-11-21)** — A second module, **Briefs** (switch `briefs_disabled`, file `briefs.json`, routes under `/admin/api/newsroom/briefs/`): topics, editions and a pool of collected headlines, collected through the day with no model (ADR-0075; plan `autonomy/morning-paper.md`). A **news search** source kind (`newssearch`, Google News RSS, headline only) for watches and briefs. The stack's model calls take turns through one gate; all readers come from one registry (`sources.Registry`). The edition itself, the screens and Telegram delivery are not built.
- **1.7 (2026-11-21)** — A source the last check could not read stays on the watch (`last_problems`, one sentence per source, cleared when it reads again), so a run that answered from the other sources is not "nothing new" in silence; the row and the dashboard card show it. A Telegram channel with no public preview says so (`ErrNoPreview`, still an `ErrShape`) and a source's name appears once in its error.
- **1.6 (2026-11-21)** — **News is recent.** A watch has `max_age` (a Go duration string, default 72 h, 1 h to 30 days; a watch saved without one reads as the default). Before the model sees anything, a post whose own date (`Item.At`) is older than the window is dropped and recorded as seen (so it never returns); a post with no date is kept. The entry keeps `posted` (the source's date) beside `at` (when it was found) and the row says "posted …" when it is set. The watch form has a "Only news from the last" picker. A row with no model headline shows a plain fallback (the first sentence, no links or emoji, at most 90 characters) and the full post when opened. The dashboard card has the period control too (the watch chips still show only from two watches).
- **1.5 (2026-11-21)** — **Headline and summary.** The model step also returns `digests: [{item, headline, summary}]` for every new item (headline at most 90 characters, summary at most 240; with `look_for` set only for items called relevant, both optional and checked against the shown items). The entry keeps `headline` and `summary` beside the original `title`, `link` and `text`; the feed row shows the headline (the title when there is none), and opening it shows the summary, the reason, the original title and the other reports. Check now says "Checking…" on the running watch's row. The feed takes `days` (1 to 30, calendar days in the reader's zone) beside `watch` and `unread`, and the page has a period select; with any filter on, Clear all / Clear read delete only the entries on screen.
- **1.4 (2026-11-21)** — **Source look-up** (ADR-0074): the watch form can look a source up before it is saved. Two admin reads, `GET /admin/api/newsroom/sources/lookup` and `.../sources/suggest`. Telegram is a name check (no keyless channel search exists); Mastodon lists trending or matching tags; a page that is not a feed offers the feeds it advertises. Answers are remembered 10 minutes (64 at most), Telegram is limited to six look-ups a minute, and the look-up never uses the timer's Telegram read slot.
- **1.3 (2026-11-21)** — **Strict filter:** with `look_for` set an item is shown only when the model says `relevant: true`; an item with no verdict is not shown and is named in the row; an answer with no verdicts, no model or a spent limit is `analysis_failed` (nothing shown or recorded, the next run retries). **Clear:** `POST /admin/api/newsroom/feed/clear` deletes entries for good (`ids`, `scope:"read"` or `scope:"all"`). **Screen:** the feed is a flat list of compact rows per day (newest day open, older days folded, "Show older days"), a select mode with Mark read and Clear, no "New" pill (the unread dot says it), details folded behind a chevron.
- **1.2 (2026-11-21)** — The relevance judgement is a **verdict on every item** (`verdicts: [{item, why, relevant}]`), not a list of the items to keep. Only `relevant: true` keeps an item; an item with no verdict is kept; the old `matches` list is no longer read. A Telegram entry no longer shows its text again under a headline that is the start of it.
- **1.1 (2026-11-20)** — "Topic" is now **Watch** everywhere (ADR-0073). A watch says what to look for (`look_for`, keywords, skip words); a keyword stage and a model relevance judgement filter new items; the first check shows up to five findings marked "First check". The page is Feed, Watches and Settings. Persisted names changed (`watches.json`). The unused Search, Paper and Section fields were removed.
- **1.0 (2026-11-20)** — First version. The Newsroom is a stack (`stacks/newsroom`, module `topics`) that follows topics, reads their sources on a timer, keeps what is new in a feed and, for a topic with a channel, hands the core a notice. The Morning Paper module, the email connector and Reddit and Bluesky sources are not built (plans: `autonomy/morning-paper.md`, `cross-cutting/email-connector.md`).

## Contract

**Watches.** A watch has a name, one to ten sources, an interval (default 1 h, floor 5 min, sent as a Go duration string), a news window `max_age` (default 72 h; older posts are skipped, see 1.6), an on/off flag and an optional channel (a connector type; empty means the feed only). It may say what to look for: `look_for` (the owner's words, at most 300 characters), `keywords` (an item must contain one) and `skip` (an item containing one is dropped), each list at most ten words of 40 characters, lower-cased and de-duplicated. A watch made by the assistant is on and feed-only and takes the same fields. Stored in one file, `watches.json`, in the stack's data folder; a factory reset empties it.

**Sources** (guarded `Fetch` only: GET, no header, 1 MiB): RSS or Atom (also GitHub releases), Hacker News search, Mastodon tag, a web page (compared by its visible text), a public Telegram channel preview (per-channel opt-in, at most one read per 10 min), a **news search** (`newssearch`: Google News RSS search, keyless; the target is the query and the instance a region such as `en-GB`, default `en-US`; every item is headline only, with its publisher moved out of the title and no text, and its link is a Google page, not the article). Each kind's target is checked when a watch is saved (`sources/validate.go`, mirrored in the form by `utils/newsroom/sourceCheck.ts`): an https address with a host for a feed or page, 1 to 200 characters for a search, a 4 to 32 character channel name, a bare tag and a plain host for Mastodon. Pasted forms are reduced to the bare value first: `https://t.me/s/aipost` or `@aipost` gives `aipost`, `#golang` gives `golang`, `https://Mastodon.social/` gives `mastodon.social`. `Fetch` itself stays strict. A source that cannot be read is `failing` when none can be, and is kept in the watch's `last_problems` when others answered; it is never silently "nothing new".

**A run** (one watch at a time; the timer loop ticks every minute, a manual run is refused while the module is off): read every source; items already in the seen ledger (60 days, 2000 per watch) are dropped; keywords and skip words drop more (recorded as seen, no model); the first successful read of a watch is a **baseline**: nothing is announced, but up to five of the items that match are put in the feed marked "First check" (with `look_for` set and no model, none); for new items the model step (the `model` port, ADR-0071) says which are new, changed (a was and now pair) or repeats of a shown recent title, and writes a short state note. When `look_for` is set the same call gives a verdict on every item (`why`, then `relevant` true or false); an item it called not relevant is recorded as seen and named in the row ("N did not match what you look for"). An item with no verdict is not shown (named in the row); an answer with no "verdicts" list, no model, or a spent daily limit when `look_for` is set is `analysis_failed`: nothing is shown or recorded and the run retries. The reason text is never read to decide. Code checks the answer: a repeat or verdict must cite a shown item or title. With no model chosen, or the daily limit (200 calls) spent, items go to the feed as new with the row "not compared". Any other model failure is `analysis_failed`: nothing is recorded and the run retries.

**Delivery.** The core words and sends the notice through the `notice` port (ADR-0072); the stack never sends. A refused channel shows `no_channel`; a failed send records nothing and retries. Every change is kept in the feed whether or not a message is sent.

**Feed.** Entries are kept 30 days, 200 per watch. The server groups entries by day then watch (newest entry `current`, the rest `earlier`); the screen reads a day as one list, newest first, and names the watch on a row only when more than one watch has entries. Repeats fold under the entry they repeat ("also seen"). Days are drawn in the zone the screen sends (`?zone=`), else UTC.

**Routes** (admin): `GET|POST /admin/api/newsroom/watches`, `PUT|DELETE .../watches/{id}`, `POST .../watches/{id}/run`, `GET .../feed` (`watch`, `unread`, `zone`), `POST .../feed/read`, `POST .../feed/clear` (`{ids}` | `{scope:"read"|"all"}`; deletes entries from storage for good; the seen ledger is kept, so a cleared item does not return from the same source), `GET .../sources/lookup` (`kind`, `target`, `instance`: what one source would read, as `{found, target, instance, title, sample[≤3], choices[], problem}`; nothing is saved; the value is checked first and a bad one is a problem with no request; a problem about the moment such as internet off is not remembered) and `GET .../sources/suggest` (`kind`, `instance`, `target`: Mastodon only, trending tags or tags matching what is typed; other kinds answer an empty list). Both are used by the watch form and the brief topic form, so they refuse only when Watches **and** Briefs are both switched off (1.12), and read only through the guarded `Fetch`. **Tools** (attended runs, `general` profile only, hidden and refused while the module is off): `newsroom_follow`, `newsroom_watches`.

**Module.** `newsroom.disabled` is the switch. Starting needs internet access allowed in Guardrails. No timer runs while the module is off.

**Screens** (SPEC-003): the Newsroom page, tabs Feed, Watches and Settings (the module switches), at `/newsroom/:tab?`; the watch form offers suggestions for what to look for, a guide per kind of source, and under each source a **Look up** that shows what it would read (name, latest items, the cleaned-up value) and offers feeds a page advertises or a Mastodon server's tags (a hint: saving does not need it); a dashboard card `newsroom-latest` that shows the latest headlines and a failing flag and links only (ADR-0064). All text is escaped; a link opens only if it is http or https.

**Briefs** (module `briefs`, ADR-0075; the news sections of an edition are built, see 1.10; the screens, watches, Home, Your day and Telegram are not): a **topic** is a name, optional category, news-search queries with a region, feeds and other readers (feed, news search, Hacker News, Mastodon; never Telegram or a page), ignore words; an **edition** is a name, a ready-by time and weekdays (read in the stack's timezone through the schedule port), how often and for how long before it collects (default every 2 h for 12 h; 1 h to 12 h, and 1 h to 24 h), and up to 12 sections, each a news section of one topic. The loop collects a topic when some enabled edition that lists it is inside its window and the topic was last collected at least that edition's interval ago (the shortest wins); **collecting never calls a model**. Collected items go into one **pool**: a story is one item with a report per outlet, found by normalised title or by link, kept three days, at most 3000. A source that cannot be read is named on its topic (`last_problems`) and cleared when it reads again. Routes (admin only, all follow the module switch where they read the internet): `GET|POST .../briefs/topics`, `PUT|DELETE .../topics/{id}`, `POST .../topics/{id}/collect` (and `POST .../editions/{id}/issue` first collects the edition's topics never read or older than its interval, 1.12), `GET|POST .../briefs/editions`, `PUT|DELETE .../editions/{id}`, `GET .../briefs/pool?topic=`, (1.12) `GET|POST .../briefs/sources` and `DELETE .../sources/{id}` (the built-in catalog then the owner's own list, `my_sources` in `briefs.json`, at most 100; each entry is `{id?, label, category, kind: feed|newssearch, target, builtin}`; a repeat of an entry in either list is refused; a topic copies the pick and keeps its `label`), and (1.10) `POST .../editions/{id}/issue`, `GET .../briefs/issues`, `GET .../issues/{id}`. Durations in these routes are strings such as `2h`. The stack's model calls (watches today, editions later) take turns through one gate and are never refused for another caller's call.
